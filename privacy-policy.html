Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Lovre respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

Information we collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide when you contact us, submit a form, respond to an advertisement, or otherwise communicate with Lovre. This may include your name, email address, company name, job information, project requirements, and any other information you choose to provide.

We may also collect basic technical and usage information when you visit our website, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and referral source, through analytics and similar technologies.

How we use your information

We may use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and project requests.
  • Evaluate potential collaborations or client engagements.
  • Provide information about Lovre's services.
  • Improve our website, marketing and user experience.
  • Measure the performance of advertising campaigns.
  • Comply with legal or regulatory requirements.

LinkedIn Lead Generation Forms

If you submit your information through a LinkedIn Lead Gen Form, the information you provide may be shared with Lovre by LinkedIn. Lovre will use this information to respond to your enquiry, understand your product or design needs, and communicate with you regarding potential services or collaboration.

LinkedIn processes your information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

Sharing of information

Lovre does not sell your personal information. We may use trusted service providers that help us operate our website, analytics, communications or marketing activities. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services.

Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to request access, correction or deletion of your data, or to object to certain processing.

To exercise these rights or ask a privacy-related question, contact:

hello@lovre.studio

Third-party services

Our website or advertising may use services provided by third parties, including LinkedIn and website analytics providers. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Lovre at:
