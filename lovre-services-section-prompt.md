Design a "services" section for a B2B design studio website called Lovre. Style: minimal, editorial, senior-consultancy feel — not a SaaS pricing grid.

LAYOUT
Single column, vertical stack. Three collapsible accordion cards, stacked with 16px gaps. All three are visually identical in weight and treatment — no card is marked "most popular," featured, or given special emphasis. All three are collapsed by default on page load. Clicking a card's header expands only that card's content, with a smooth height transition and a chevron icon that rotates 180° on open.

VISUAL STYLE
- Outer section background: ivory, #FAF8F4
- Card background: white, #FFFFFF
- Card border: 1px solid #E4E1D6, border-radius 10px, padding 20px 24px
- Headings: Fraunces (serif)
- Body copy: Inter (sans-serif)
- Accent color: deep teal, #0E4F4F — used for the meta label (price/format) and the chevron icon
- Body/description text: #6B6759

SECTION HEADER (above the cards)
Eyebrow label: "01 — services" — small caps, uppercase, teal (#0E4F4F), letter-spacing 0.08em
Heading: "Three ways to work together." — Fraunces, ~30px, regular weight

CARD 1 (collapsed by default)
Header (always visible): "Not sure what's actually broken?"
Expanded content:
- Meta label (teal): "UX audit — 2 weeks"
- Description: "A structured review of your product's most critical workflows — what works, what breaks, and where to fix it first."

CARD 2 (collapsed by default)
Header (always visible): "Know you need embedded design support?"
Expanded content:
- Meta label (teal): "Product design partner — monthly retainer"
- Description: "An embedded senior designer working alongside your product and engineering team — discovery, interaction design, and design QA on a sustained cadence."

CARD 3 (collapsed by default)
Header (always visible): "Know your design system needs rebuilding?"
Expanded content:
- Meta label (teal): "Design system and modernization — 3–6 months"
- Description: "A structured engagement to audit, build, or modernize your product's design system — component library, tokens, documentation, and dev handoff."

INTERACTION NOTES
- No card should be open by default.
- No visual hierarchy implying an order or a required first step — each card is an equally valid, independent entry point.
- Only one open card at a time is fine, but not required — either behavior works as long as all three start closed.
